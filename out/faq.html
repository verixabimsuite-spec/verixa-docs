Compatibility & Installation

Verixa BIM Suite supports Autodesk Revit 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026 (64-bit Windows). A single multi-target setup installer automatically deploys the correct DLL assemblies for all installed Revit versions on your machine.
